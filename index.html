how it works

  1. brief your agent

    Upload the coin's picture, name it, write the brief and hire one of five agents. Give it a test shift before you launch.

  2. launch on pump.fun

    You sign once in your own wallet. The fee split is locked in the same signatures: 90% to you, 10% to the house, which pays for the agents.

  3. it works shifts

    Every six hours while the coin trades, your agent writes a post, builds a raid kit and makes a meme from the coin's picture.

  4. you post it

    Every piece of bagwork has a one-click post to X and copy buttons. The agent writes, the community ships.

what is bagworker?

A launchpad where every coin comes with an AI bagworker agent. You launch on pump.fun; your agent does the bagwork for it every shift: a post, a raid kit and a meme made from the coin's own picture.

does the agent post on X by itself?

No. It writes and makes everything, and every piece has a one-click post to X that opens with the text ready. You and your community decide what goes out.

how are the memes made?

With FLUX through Vercel's AI Gateway, using the coin's picture as the reference so the mascot stays itself. The writing comes from a small OpenAI model in the agent's style.

how often does it work?

One shift right after launch, then one every six hours while the coin trades. A day without a trade and it slows down; seven days and it stops. One trade puts it back on shift. The house has a daily meme budget; if it runs out, shifts resume at 00:00 UTC.

who gets the fees?

pump.fun's own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that launch the coin: 90% to you, 10% to the house, which pays for the agents. Before you sign, the page reads the transactions and refuses to go on if the split isn't the one shown.

what won't the agent write?

No financial advice, no price predictions, no promises of gains, never "buy" or "sell", no real people, nothing sexual. Anything that slips through is cut before it's shown.

does bagworker hold my keys?

No. You sign everything in your own wallet and the server has no key. The house is a public address; its tenth arrives through pump.fun's split.
